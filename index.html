About PACS Studio.

PACS Studio is a full-service design, construction, and visualization studio. We combine architecture, interior design, advanced 3D rendering, and hands-on project execution under one roof.

Our integrated approach ensures seamless continuity between early conceptual designs, photorealistic client visualizations, and the final physical build.
